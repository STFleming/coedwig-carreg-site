  • House Mulbrek

    Holds the fortified gate at the foot of the Falls of Nyf . Their guards take a toll from any traveller going upriver. Barney McGrue 's rendition of Take On Me was enough to get the whole party through on their way up the River Hameth . First encountered: Session 1.

  • The Drune

    An old order of occultists. Their sign is the owl, which is why owls are counted a bad omen in Dolmenwood. The party found a dead Drune in the upper levels of Droomen Knoll , drained of blood through small puncture wounds.
