Prince Mallowheart

A fey prince of the Wood: slender, cruel. Clement knows him from his dreams, and his face is on a mural in the corridor beneath Droomen Knoll, under the inscription:

On the slow creep of eons, Mallowheart's mercy may shine.

His mortal daughter is Lady-heart-torn-in-tatters, depicted in the frost-chamber beside him with her face turned away. Around him in the same room: Lord Slumberbone, two unnamed elves under the inscription "Prince 7 Past Noon", Lady Silver So Mirrored, a frost-clad knight of the Cold Prince, and Whisper of Candle Light.

Famine Whistle Wither says he used to serve Mallowheart, but was imprisoned for a cooking offence. He also says Mallowheart killed the elf courtier whose skull was found in the cell next to his own.

Two redcaps, Nicklethumb and Jack O'Boots, were looking for what was taken from Mallowheart: the chalice the party retrieved from the icy basin at Droomen Knoll. The redcaps were also carrying a human infant as a delivery to "Nana", and said Mallowheart is trying to make a deal.
