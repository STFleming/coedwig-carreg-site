Session 2 : Beneath the Knoll

The breggle ambush and the descent into the knoll's undercroft

Weather: Hot and Humid

The Breggle Ambush ¶

  • "Get Grognot!" screams a female breggle as she emerges from the top-right tower brandishing pots and pans
  • Barney McGrue decides to flee back to the main group
  • Fennic Pouncemouse swings his mace and drops the first breggle
  • Arrows fly and swords slash against armour all around
  • Barney McGrue is bludgeoned from behind by a pursuing breggle
  • To everyone's surprise, winged creatures fly in and start attacking indiscriminately
  • Oblom Grimehump falls to an attack
  • Eventually the breggles and flying creatures are slain
  • The party pulls Barney McGrue and Oblom Grimehump to their feet and proceed into the courtyard, pan pipes echoing on the wind
  • The fire in the centre shows the breggles have been hunting and eating Woodgrue
  • As a group they head down the stairs, following the sound of the music

The Undercroft ¶

  • On the next level down, crushed and killed breggles lie limply on the damp flagstones — including the one from upstairs with the two pans
  • In the corner, a two-headed cockerell-type creature sleeps, tied to a door
  • Fennic Pouncemouse holds position on the stairs to avoid making sound while the rest sneak around the corner into the corridor
  • They find an ornate sundial with a glowing gnome
  • Behind it, an elven figure is inscribed on the wall with the words: "On the slow creep of eons Mallowheart's mercy may shine"
  • Clement Dunwallow recognises the elf from his dreams — Prince Mallowheart
  • In one of the rooms, loose bones and scratching on the floor
  • Ahead, the pan pipes continue playing

Thinwhistle ¶

  • Now joined by Fennic Pouncemouse, the group forms up at a door and opens it
  • Beyond: a bar-like prison cell inhabited by a ragged elven man with wild tangled hair, playing pan pipes carved from rat bones
  • "Oh my goodness! Hello! Other people — not breggle fiends!" He scans across the group and stops on Clement Dunwallow, recognising him. Clement does not recognise him.
  • The elf names himself Thinwhistle and says he has been wrongly imprisoned...

Session 2 ends with Thinwhistle's introduction, continuing into Session 3.
