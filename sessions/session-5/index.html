Session 5 : The Road to Dreg

The road north from the knoll, an inn, and a redcap ambush

Weather: Hot and muggy, St Foggarty (Feast of the Woodcutters)

The party headed north from Droomen Knoll through tangled woodland with Arda Vague, the infant (carried by Clement), and the two captive redcaps Nicklethumb and Jack O'Boots. Oblom spotted tracks of four moss moles and tried to tame one with rations but was unsuccessful. They got lost in the forest but Oblom found his way.

Trader in the tree ¶

Arriving at Harrid's Path, an untrodden road surrounded by tall birch trees, the party saw a shadowy figure talking to the bottom of a large birch tree. The figure was an emancipated Drune wearing the same clothes and owl torc as the dead drune found in Droomen Knoll. He was addled, wanted Devil's Grease fungus, and stormed off into the woods when he found the party couldn't help.

The tree the Drune was talking to contained a small fey creature who introduced himself as Dreebe, a scrabey trader. He had a tap-like nose that could pour mead, telescopic fingers, retractable fingernails, and a large backpack that functioned as a mobile dwelling. He offered trade goods including a fuzzy lilac moss called Sclubbers Twist (great visions for demi-fey but horrible for mortals), radishes, and firewood. Barney bought a helping of Sclubbers Twist for 110 gp.

Dreebe wanted the location of the King of the Woodgrue's court (King Ogglewoop) for business. Barney knew but was hesitant. Dreebe gave information about Dreg being in trouble with violence and petty theft. Barney asked about mushroom gathering nearby and Dreebe told them about Bogwitt Manor — overgrown with all sorts of fungus. Something is wrong there. Dreebe believes death is feeding these fungi and it's getting out of hand. He's not sure it's safe anymore to go.

The House of the Harridwn ¶

The party arrived at The House of the Harridwn, a small run-down inn on the path with a forlorn barn to the side. Oblom, Ghrend, and Barney inspected the barn first. It's designed for keeping horses and mules overnight, with basic maintenance equipment around, but no evidence of recent use — no dung, no manure. A door off the back leads to a small clearing. They fetched the others and secured the redcaps. Fennic and Clement stayed behind to guard the captives and the infant, while Arda stayed in the barn. The redcaps were deeply agitated to be moved so close to a human settlement.

Inside, Mallowyn and Trellayde Occland, elderly twin sisters, welcomed them and served Puggle Pie (1 sp), Hammeth Foam ale (1 sp per pint), and currant buns for free. Ghrend had 6 pints.

The party smoked Burglar's Blend and discussed Grognott's map. They realised Scrag and Bert are not just names of killed Crockhorns but names of different spots on the map. The Crockhorns travelled from the north through Droomen Knoll with Dreg as their final destination. There must be a connection between Dreg's troubles and the Crockhorns.

Mallowyn and Trellayde said they have a guest upstairs: Mr Rumbleow with three children who keep to themselves. During the stay, the party heard an infant crying from upstairs, then later a knocking sound. The innkeepers recommended the Mermaid's Arms in Dreg (posh, locals call it the Mermaid's Arse), warned against Shogerty's Shack in the port, and mentioned Madam Shantywood's place across the Hameth river by shuttle boat.

RedCap(tured) ¶

Leaving the inn, Arda Vague told the party the road's name and that it leads to the Ditchway where he would part ways. He refused to go to Dreg and told them to contact his mother, a barmaid in Shoggerty's Shack.

The captive redcaps complained they needed their ritual dance or they'd die. Obolm spotted something red in the hedge ahead. Two more redcaps emerged from the bushes: Thimblefiddle in a red capotain, and Old Henry in a red flat cap. They began singing in unison with the captive redcaps:

Redcaps round the bramble, pockets full of ramble, Hush now, Hush now, Close your weary eyes.

Ghrend shot Thimblefiddle in the shoulder with an arrow, the capotain tumbling to the floor. The others shouted "Thimblefiddle nooo." The redcaps sang again:

Baby in the basket, Don't you dare to ask it, Who cries, Who cries, Under Droomen Knoll.

Fennic charged and stabbed Thimblefiddle with his spear. Blood gushed and touched the capotain hat on the ground. Croaking and coughing blood, Thimblefiddle began to sing and the others joined in:

Hush now little sparrow, Dreams grow deep and narrow, Step soft, step soft, Down the mossy stairs.

As the nursery rhyme ended, all the mortals (Oblom, Clement, and Arda) suddenly felt dizzy and woozy and fell to the floor deeply asleep. Ghrend resisted.

As Thimblefiddle finished the last note, Barney hit him with a sling shot to the face, killing him. Old Henry rushed to free Nicklethumb and succeeded before Fennic's spear pierced him. Nicklethumb grabbed Old Henry's club and fought back but was killed by Fennic's spear. Ghrend shot an arrow at Jack O'Boots that pierced his tricorn hat beside his head.

The session ended with the party standing over the captive Jack O'Boots, who promised to do whatever they want.

Treasure this session: 145 gp from Thimblefiddle, blue spinel gem (500 gp) from Old Henry. Total: 645 gp.
