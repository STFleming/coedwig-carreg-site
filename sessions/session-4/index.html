Session 4 : Redcaps at the Knoll

The night at the ruined keep and the road to the north-west

Weather: Hot and muggy, New Moon

Scouting the Keep ¶

  • The party spends the evening at the top of Droomen Knoll, inspecting the ruined keep and preparing to camp
  • Fennic Pouncemouse and Barney McGrue scout the grounds for safety
  • Oblom Grimehump climbs the tower and surveys the surroundings:
  • North: A thick canopy rising above them
  • North-west: A small inn-like dwelling with a road cutting beneath the canopy, surrounded by birch trees
  • South-west: Dense forest with a clearing showing evidence of a stone circle
  • South: The River Hameth falling into the Falls of Nyf
  • North-east: Beyond the river, a manor house with large bulbous towers

Arda's Story ¶

  • Clement Dunwallow comforts the young lad they rescued from Grognot's chamber
  • The boy gives his name: Arda Vague
  • He was caught stealing a fish in Dreg, and Brother Hogbeard's zealots were going to perform an exorcism on him in the woods outside town
  • He punched Hogbeard and escaped, wandering for days — strangely feeling better away from Dreg
  • He was heading for Lankshorn but got lost and found the keep with "those disgusting breggles"
  • He also mentions Madam Shantywood, who runs the Shantywood Isles and is almost the real ruler of Dreg

Camp Finds ¶

  • Searching the campsite, Oblom finds 20 smokes of Burglar's Blend pipeleaf
  • Barney McGrue carefully moves some rancid bedding and discovers a bronze statue of a dancing nymph (50 gp)
  • The party examines Grognott's map and a letter mentioning Rodger Burl, a member of the Dreg Constabulary being paid by Grognot's boss to turn a blind eye to certain happenings within Dreg

Night Watch ¶

  • The party camps — Oblom starts a fire, Clement cooks a delicious meal from rations, Barney plays rousing music
  • Everyone has an excellent night's sleep and takes watch
  • On the last watch, Barney sees movement
  • As Oblom is falling asleep, he whispers to the roots, asking if any danger is approaching
  • The roots reply: "BEWARE OF RED CAPS."

The Redcaps ¶

  • Two redcaps approach — one wearing a red bowler hat, the other a tricorn
  • Barney blows his bone whistle to wake the others; stirges fly rapidly out of the hole in the keep
  • The redcaps shout to each other
  • The one with the tricorn is Jack O'Boots
  • The one with the bowler hat is Nicklethumb, holding a wicker basket
  • They are looking for what was taken from Prince Mallowheart: the chalice

The Ambush ¶

  • The party convince the redcaps the chalice is still downstairs
  • Jack O'Boots stays with the party while Nicklethumb goes to check
  • Barney tells a riveting story of their adventures to Jack, who is mesmerised — particularly by the tale of Thinwhistle
  • Meanwhile:
  • Oblom Grimehump and Ghrend Underbleat sneak behind Jack
  • Clement Dunwallow sneaks up to the wicker basket containing the infant
  • Fennic Pouncemouse and Barney McGrue move to ambush Nicklethumb coming up the stairs
  • Clement grabs the infant from the basket and runs out the entrance
  • Oblom and Ghrend attack Jack O'Boots but fail — Jack hits back with his club and knocks out Oblom
  • Eventually the party damages Jack enough that he surrenders
  • While they tie Jack up, Clement heals Oblom
  • Nicklethumb comes up the stairs; Fennic and the rest engage him, dealing considerable damage until he also surrenders

Morning ¶

  • It is early morning on the 4th of Lillipythe — the day of Moot
  • Also the Feast of St Foggarty, an important feast day for the Woodcutters
  • The party interrogate the redcaps:
  • Where they came from and where they got the infant
  • The redcaps say they were taking it to "Nana"
  • Mallowheart is trying to make a deal
  • They got the infant from outside the human settlement to the north

Into the Forest ¶

  • The party decides to head into the forest to the north-west, toward the inn that Oblom spotted and the small road
  • Arda is pleased — he really doesn't want to go back to Dreg
  • They make their way through the tangled woodland with Arda, the infant (carried by Clement), and the two redcaps tied up
  • Oblom spots tracks of four moss moles and tries to tame one with rations — unsuccessful
  • They get lost in the tangled forest but Oblom is able to find his way

The Road ¶

  • Arriving at the road, the party sees a shadowy figure talking to the bottom of a tree by the roadside, gesturing irritably
  • Oblom asks the roots what he is saying; the roots reply: "WANT MUSHROOMS."
  • Barney McGrue uses his large woodgrue ears to listen in:
  • The shadowy figure wants Devil's Grease fungus
  • Something in the tree doesn't have any anymore
  • They'd need to go to Bogwitt Manor — things there are "getting outta hand"
  • They want to visit the King of the Woodgrue and are asking for directions

Treasure this session: 50 gp.
