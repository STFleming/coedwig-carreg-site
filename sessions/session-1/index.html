Session 1 : Up the Falls of Nyf

Journeying into Dolmenwood up the River Hameth

Weather: Hot and Humid

The Greasy Eel ¶

  • The party travels aboard the Greasy Eel, captained by Goodjen Filch, up the River Hameth
  • A deafening roar drowns out all other sound — a massive waterfall ahead
  • At the base of the falls, a fortified wooden gate operated by House Mulbrek guards demands a toll
  • Barney McGrue performs "Take On Me" to persuade them. The guards love it and open the gate for free.
  • Goodjen Filch becomes enamoured with Barney McGrue and complains about how bad Dreg has become. He recommends the Shantywood Isles nearby.

Signs on the Shore ¶

  • As they progress upriver, they spot a Featureless Tower on the shore — home to the mad wizard who created the Falls of Nyf
  • To the east, a ruin on a hill that Clement Dunwallow recognises from a dream (it was covered in ice)
  • The group decides to investigate

Droomen Knoll ¶

  • As they disembark, faint music carries on the air — drums and pan pipes, growing louder as they approach the knoll
  • Atop the knoll stands a ruined tower: Droomen Knoll
  • A figure at the top of the tower has not yet noticed them
  • Oblom Grimehump and Ghrend Underbleat stealth their way up toward the tower and spot three goat-like figures dancing around a fire, preparing food
  • Three breggles — gnarled and scary-looking compared to a normal, respectable Breggle
  • Half the group heads right to explore the ruined tower while Fennic Pouncemouse and Clement Dunwallow hold the middle
  • In the ruined tower, they find a small 2 ft hole that drops into a 10 ft drop
  • The breggles spot the group and prepare to attack
  • Fennic Pouncemouse steps up and braces himself for the fight...

Session 1 ends mid-combat, continuing into Session 2.
