Session 6: The Vibe Was Damp and Racist

Players: Ghrend Underbleat, Fennic Pouncemouse, Oblom Grimehump, Barney McGrue, Clement Dunwallow Date at start: Moot, 4th Lillipythe Weather at start: Hot and Humid

Dry Your Cap Mate ¶

  • The gang make their way up Harrid's Path and onto The Ditchway (main road between Lankshorn and Dreg)
  • Where the path joins the Ditchway Arda Vague leaves to head to Lankshorn.
    • He asks the party for some money, Fennic Pouncemouse give him 2GP
    • He also asks the party to find his mother Melysse Vague, who works as a barmaid in Shodgerey's Shack and to tell her that he has made his way to Lankshorn.
  • As they get closer and closer to Dreg, Jack O'Boots becomes increasingly agitated.
  • He tries multiple times to pull away and dive into the dense forest along the road but Barney McGrue has him on a tight leash and is able to hold him.
  • The see Dreg before them, in the evening sun it looks like the entire town is slumping into the River Hameth. All the buildings look slightly run down and are painted with a thick black tar to protect against the damp.
  • As they cross the city line Jack O'Boots drags his heels, but Barney McGrue and Fennic Pouncemouse pull him over the line.
  • Jack O'Boots looks dazed but holds his bound hands up to his cap, he takes it off his head, and wrings it out. Red blood flows out from his hat onto The Ditchway and the hat turns blue. He places it back onto his head and then falls into a deep sleep.
  • Fennic Pouncemouse picks up Jack O'Boots and carries him, cradling him like a baby.
  • As they enter Dreg three drunk farmhands are walking by a statue in a fenced pool, they appear to be bragging about hunting a witch as they are walking north.
  • Barney McGrue calls out to them, they start to jeer and make rude comments at the non-human / Breggle members of the party.
  • They eventually disappear to the north as Barney McGrue manages to intimidate them.

The Mermaids Arse ¶

  • It's getting late so the party decide to enter The Mermaids Arms
  • There is a sign: "No gambling, lewdness, or sea shanties!"
  • They are greeted by Ogham Tweede
  • There are rooms available for the night
    • 3 shared rooms (4sp each)
  • On the menu is:
    • Snail skewers: Forest snails skewered on metal spikes and roast over a wood fire. Served with a dipping custard.
    • Tweede's fish pie: best in Dreg
    • Side: pickled eggs
    • The beer on tap is Barrowblaster A robust ale, streaked black and white, with a rich, iron-like flavour. Effect: Belching and goggling.
  • The gang have a meal for 1 gp + 5 sp
  • Fennic Pouncemouse has a saucer of milk.
  • In the corner of the common room there is an elderly lady reading a book and occasionally peering over at the gang, she has a resemblance to Ogham Tweede.
  • As he is bringing out the food, drinks, and smokes, Ogham Tweede asks the adventurers if they have ever encountered anyone on their travels called One eyed Gil. This was an old flame of his. They went on a daring adventure to rob Lord Malbleat at RedWraith Manor to steal some of his occult artefacts (he's into that sort of thing), but they were chased away by two Lankston Mastiffs. They didn't manage to steal from Lord Malbleat but One eyed Gil stole Ogham Tweede's heart, he'd do anything for information on her.
  • He shows the gang a rough map of Dreg and points out the highlights. He also mentions that there has been a lot of trouble in Dreg lately.
  • The party ask about the farm lads giving them trouble outside, Ogham Tweede explains that there have been a lot of signs around town lately. Pasted image 20260523102113.png
  • He shows them one with the symbol above on it and written "Get THEM out of Dreg".
  • Ogham Tweede explains that they have been blaming all the problems in Dreg on the mystical nature of the woods,The Drune, Fey, Witches, are all to blame for why Dreg has gone to shit.
  • The gang decides to send the human Clement Dunwallow and Breggle Ghrend Underbleat out for an evening stroll while Oblom Grimehump and Barney McGrue enjoy smokes of "Shaggy Pony" which all smells a bit horsey but makes Oblom and Barney feel quite sleepy as they contemplate all that has happened.

An Evening Stroll ¶

Ghrend Underbleat and Clement Dunwallow go for a circular walk around the town. The town seems to be full of empty, gaudy barber shops and pipeleaf stores.

  • They visit the following locations
  • The Smokehouse : quiet dark, thick dirty acrid smoke smell coming from the chimney.
  • The Spawning Salmon : lively and hopping, cheering and whooping both outside and inside around it, the pair go inside
    • Many gambling tables.
    • Upstairs terrace, with people looking down on the gambling tables
    • A stage (currently empty)
    • Smell of grilled meats
  • Ghrend Underbleat talks to the landlord, a tall wiry human in his late forties with Bulging eyes and long greasy hair. He is perpetually rolling up his sleeves.
    • Ghrend Underbleat has a pint of cider, Clement Dunwallow has a water.
    • Landlord alludes to enjoyable thievery
    • Ghrend Underbleat hold up a sign that has been seen around town, the landlord seems horrified and perplexed that a Breggle would want to hang out with those farm hands. The vibe becomes a bit uncomfortable and they leave quickly.
  • Church of St Wick : It appears to be shut and mouldering from the outside, looks very run down
    • They can see the port, where they can see the free boats transporting patrons to Chateau Shantywood with red lights on them. They also see some larger boats making their way to the woodcutters encampment up river.
    • A little down the way they can see a friar shouting, holding up The Chapes and telling the people of Dreg to repent. He is surrounded by yellow smocked followers, standing firmly with their arms folded. The pair decide to avoid that situation and go back along the south of town to The Mermaids Arms.

Blue Cap ¶

The gang go to sleep for the night in the comfort of The Mermaids Arms. In the morning when they wake, Jack O'Boots seems to have completely changed.

  • He seems helpful, friendly and cooperative.
  • He tells them he stole the child from a woman when she was just on the outskirts of Dreg
  • She had gappy teeth, a pot belly, and brown cropped hair.

They enjoy a nice continental breakfast downstairs, selection of cured bogswine sausages and meats. They ask if their equipment is safe and Ogham Tweede says that they can keep their stuff safely here if they pay for a nights stay upfront (which they do).

"Squire Fennic! Squire Fennic!" ¶

As they leave The Mermaids Arms they can hear a voice calling out to Fennic Pouncemouse. It is a young messenger from House Harrowmoor called Jassper.

  • He's been worried sick waiting for Fennic who was supposed to be here days ago.
  • He has a letter for Fennic from Lady Harrowmoor.
  • She is concerned about Crookhorns appearing further south and has heard rumour about sightings near Dreg.
  • She asks Fennic Pouncemouse to bring as much evidence as she can, she needs to convince the other noble houses that this is a real growing threat.
  • As the only Fey member of her court she may also need him to convince other members of the Fey to join in their fight, or make them aware, desperate times.
  • Jassper mentions that things have been fraught at House Harrowmoor
  • Lady Harrowmoor's daughter Violet is missing, they have no idea where she is.

Fennic asks Jassper for money, the young lad has nothing left and is sick of Dreg and leaves back for House Harrowmoor.

A cat among the rubble ¶

They decide that they should visit Berkmaster Manor to visit Berkmaster Maximilian Monocleese and inform him about the missing child. As they approach there are the same three farm hands from the previous night staring at the party as they stand outside the Berkmaster Manor. They are jeering at Berkmaster Manor shouting that "he's murdering his wife". Barney McGrue calls out to them and the three of them skulk away to the north. Fennic Pouncemouse transforms into his chester form (a fat domestic cat) and chases after them.

  • He follows them up to Burnt out farmhouse on the outskirts of Dreg towards the bogswine farms.
  • He can see them make their way into the rubble.
  • He stalks into the abandoned house and can see bootprints and a subtle path to one corner where there is a carefully arranged pile of rubble.

The Rt Hon Berk ¶

While Fennic Pouncemouse stalked the farm hands the rest of the gang tries to get into Berkmaster Manor. The manor house is crazy, raised high up on stilts and appears to be facing Chateau Shantywood.

A member of the Dreg Constabulary stop them, until Ghrend Underbleat mentions that they have information regarding Crookhorns. They are allowed into visit Berkmaster Maximilian Monocleese.

Inside they see Berkmaster Maximilian Monocleese sitting behind a desk. He asks them how he can help them:

  • They mention the missing child they found.
  • He sighs, missing children cases are managed by a specific member of Dreg Constabulary a man named Rodger Burl.
  • He doesn't know where Rodger Burl is right now, but can fetch for him, if they come back at 4pm today he will try to make sure he is here.
  • As the party talk to him, he mentions that his wife is unwell.
  • They offer to take a look, perhaps Clement Dunwallow is able to heal
  • Hilda Monocleese is clearly very unwell in bed upstairs
  • Ghrend Underbleat is able to identify that this must be some kind of poison, this comes as a great surprise to Berkmaster Maximilian Monocleese and he is thankful for the information.
