Session 3 : Grognot's Fall

Freeing Thinwhistle, the frost room, and the boss fight

Weather: Hot and Humid

Thinwhistle's Story ¶

  • "Have you been up to no good fighting them? Could you get me out of here? I will pay you well?"
  • He is called Thinwhistle, works for Prince Mallowheart, and was imprisoned for cooking something wrong

The Adjacent Cell ¶

  • In the cell next door, Clement Dunwallow sees the cell where he was kept in his dream
  • A Drune was scratching away in the corner
  • Elven script scratched into the wall reads:
  • "Mallow heart be curse"
  • "Famine whistle be doubly cursed"
  • "Hound baker"
  • They take the skull. Barney McGrue asks Thinwhistle about the skeleton — he was an elf courtier apparently killed by Prince Mallowheart as well

The Frost Room ¶

  • At the top right of the corridor, Clement Dunwallow sees an ornate room with a large table and six alcoves, each with a faded mural
  • Magical frost covers everything in the room
  • Murals and inscriptions:
  • A portly fey noble — "Lord Slumberbone"
  • Beneath the table, another mural
  • Smashed doors with carvings of icy tree branches
  • Beyond the smashed doors: a destroyed room with crumbling plaster and guano — likely the roost of the winged creatures that attacked from above
  • In the next room, a dead body covered in puncture wounds, drained of blood
  • Oblom Grimehump investigates and finds:
  • An owl torque (150 gp) — a bad omen in Dolmenwood
  • A scroll sealed with a silver eye-shaped ring (750 gp)
  • Barney McGrue recognises it as a Scroll of Decipher
  • An orange gem (50 gp) — when touched, Barney has a vision of a twitching tar-coated stag corpse surrounded by trilithon stones: "Ill omens at the withing stone, winter cometh"
  • They identify the body as a Drune

The Table Murals ¶

  • Returning to the frosty room, the group moves the table and sees beautifully carved scenes of elves hunting
  • Additional murals:
  • Three bright red hounds (appearing added after the fact), yipping at the feet of a slender elf with scraggly hair playing a tin whistle — "Famine Whistle Wither"
  • An evil-looking elf whom Clement Dunwallow recognises as Prince Mallowheart, flanked by two elves looking away — "Prince 7 Past Noon"
  • A slender elven woman with a mirror — "Lady Silver So Mirrored"
  • A frost-clad knight
  • A statuesque woman holding sword and shield — "Whisper of Candle Light"

The Sundial ¶

  • The group shines their torch on the sundial at 7 past noon
  • A grinding noise echoes down the corridor and the pan pipes grow loud
  • Frost rushes down the corridor toward Thinwhistle
  • Thinwhistle approaches, happy to be freed and doubly happy he doesn't owe them anything for it
  • He marches past and excitedly opens the door to Grognot's chamber

Grognot ¶

  • Inside: a 7-foot corrupted Crockhorn with knarled horns, wearing a heavy greasy fur cloak, glowing eyes, surrounded by noxious yellow candles that he breathes in deeply
  • In the corner: a youthful human male, bound with rope, staring into space and twitching
  • Smashed crates litter the room
  • The encounter begins — Grognot smashes into Thinwhistle
  • Fennic Pouncemouse engages in melee
  • Barney McGrue tries to knock over the candles
  • Oblom Grimehump checks on the bound human in the corner
  • Ghrend Underbleat takes position at the door, shooting arrows into Grognot — a lucky shot hits him right in the eye, dropping him to the floor

Aftermath ¶

  • Thinwhistle, shaken after the attack, is interrogated
  • Asked about the killing of the hounds and the location of the Whything Stones
  • He says the Cold Prince's "daughter" (a mortal) would meet at the Whything Stones — a few hexes north
  • He leaves, killing the monster barring the exit
  • Barney McGrue finds a reinforced barred chest and a false wall
  • Beyond the false wall: another room with high vaulted ceilings, cold with gentle snowfall from the ceiling
  • In the centre, a 10 ft stone basin with stag skull carvings around the edge
  • Fennic Pouncemouse leaps onto the edge and sees a beautiful chalice beneath the ice
  • Each time he hacks at the ice with his spear, the chalice starts to disappear
  • Clement Dunwallow has left the dungeon with the bound human, who is starting to recover
  • Murals around the edges show elves killing humans and draining their blood into a lake
  • Clement Dunwallow returns — he recognises the chalice from his dream: a person was chasing him, trying to get his blood
  • He drops his blood into the basin, the ice melts, and he reaches in to grab the icy chalice
  • Inside: a strange liquid (2000 gp) that pulsates with colours and smells like his favourite drink
  • Barney McGrue searches Grognot and contracts public lice, but finds a note and the key for the nearby chest
  • The chest contains:
  • One sack of 200 silver pieces
  • One sack of 500 gp
  • A pair of amber-studded bracelets (750 gp each = 1500 gp)
  • A note in Ghrend Underbleat's language
  • A mechanism disengages when the chest is opened

Arda Vague ¶

  • The human awakes fully — he is Arda Vague, from Dreg, a nearby settlement
  • Brother Hogbeard's zealots captured him after he was caught stealing
  • Dreg has become lawless; these zealots have been taking the law into their own hands
  • He escaped by punching Hogbeard and wandering into the forest, only to be found by the breggles and taken down here

The party decides to camp by the fire for the night before heading to Dreg the next day.

Treasure this session: 4970 gp. XP: 10 xp each for killing Grognot.
