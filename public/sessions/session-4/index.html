Session 4 : Redcaps at the Knoll

The night at the ruined keep and the road to the north-west

Weather: Hot and muggy, New Moon

The party spent the evening at the top of Droomen Knoll, inspecting the ruined keep and preparing to camp. Fennic and Barney scouted the grounds for safety. Obolm climbed the tower and surveyed the surroundings: to the north, a thick canopy rising above them; to the north-west, a small inn-like dwelling with a road cutting beneath the canopy surrounded by birch trees; to the south-west, a dense forest with a clearing showing evidence of a stone circle; to the south, the river Hameth falling into the Falls of Nyf; and to the north-east, beyond the river, a manor house with large bulbous towers.

Clement comforted the young lad they had rescued from Grognot's chamber. The boy gave his name: Arda Vague. He told them he was caught stealing a fish in Dreg, and that Brother Hogbeard's zealots were going to perform an exorcism on him in the woods outside town. He punched Hogbeard and escaped, wandering for days, strangely feeling better away from Dreg. He was heading for Lankshorn but got lost and found the keep with those disgusting breggles. He also told them about Madam Shantywood, who runs the Shantywood Isles and is almost the real ruler of Dreg.

Searching the campsite, Oblom found 20 smokes of Burglar's Blend pipeleaf. Barney carefully moved some rancid bedding and discovered a bronze statue of a dancing nymph worth 50gp. The party also examined Grognott's map and a letter mentioning Rodger Burl, a member of the Dreg Constabulary being paid by Grognot's boss to turn a blind eye to certain happenings within Dreg.

The party camped. Oblom started a fire, Clement cooked a delicious meal from rations, and Barney played rousing music. Everyone had an excellent night's sleep and took watch. On the last watch, Barney saw movement. As Oblom was falling asleep, he whispered to the roots asking if any danger was approaching. The roots replied: "BEWARE OF RED CAPS."

Two redcaps approached, one wearing a red bowler hat, the other a tricorn. Barney blew his bone whistle to wake the others, and stirges flew rapidly out of the hole in the keep. The redcaps shouted to each other. The one with the tricorn was Jack O'Boots, and the one with the bowler hat was Nicklethumb, who was holding a wicker basket. They were looking for what was taken from Prince Mallowheart: the chalice.

The party convinced them the chalice was still downstairs. Jack O'Boots stayed with the party while Nicklethumb went to check. Barney told a riveting story of their adventures to Jack, who was mesmerised, particularly about Thinwhistle. Meanwhile, Oblom and Ghrend sneaked behind Jack, Clement sneaked up to the wicker basket containing the infant, and Fennic and Barney moved to ambush Nicklethumb coming up the stairs.

Clement grabbed the infant from the basket and ran out the entrance. Oblom and Ghrend attacked Jack O'Boots but failed. Jack hit back with his club and knocked out Obolm. Eventually the party damaged Jack enough that he surrendered. While they tied Jack up, Clement healed Obolm. Nicklethumb came up the stairs and Fennic and the rest engaged him, dealing considerable damage until he also surrendered.

It was early morning on the 4th of Lillipythe, the day of Moot. It was also the Feast of St Foggarty, an important feast day for the Woodcutters. The party asked the redcaps where they came from and where they got the infant. The redcaps said they were taking it to "Nana", that Mallowheart is trying to make a deal, and that they got the infant from outside the human settlement to the north.

The party decided to head into the forest to the north-west towards the inn that Oblom spotted and the small road. Arda was pleased, he really doesn't want to go back to Dreg. They made their way through the tangled woodland with Arda, the infant (carried by Clement), and the two redcaps tied up. Oblom spotted tracks of four moss moles and tried to tame one with rations but was unsuccessful. They got lost in the tangled forest but Oblom was able to find his way.

Arriving at the road, the party saw a shadowy figure talking to the bottom of a tree by the roadside, gesturing irritably. Oblom asked the roots what he was saying, and the roots replied: "WANT MUSHROOMS." Barney used his large woodgrue ears to listen in: the shadowy figure wants Devil's Grease fungus. Something in the tree doesn't have any anymore. They'd need to go to Bogwitt Manor, and things there are "getting outta hand." They want to visit the King of the Woodgrue and are asking for directions.

50gp found this session.
