Mallowyn and Trellayde Occland

Elderly twin sisters who run The House of the Harridwn on Harrid's Path. Both have bright blue eyes, grey hair, and wear tidy knitwear of different colours.

Welcomed the party and offered Puggle Pie (1 sp), Hammeth Foam ale (1 sp per pint), and currant buns for free. Said their family has been in the home for many generations.

Said they have a guest upstairs: Mr Rumbleow with three children who keep to themselves.

Gave the party recommendations for Dreg: - The Mermaid's Arms: a nice spot, though locals find it posh and call it the Mermaid's Arse - Shogerty's Shack in the port: they would avoid it - Madam Shantywood's place across the Hameth river: accessed by shuttle boat

Encountered: Session 5.
