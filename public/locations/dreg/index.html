Dreg

A settlement on the upper River Hameth, the party's next destination. Goodjen Filch doesn't think much of it ("how shitty the town has become") and recommends the The Shantywood Isles instead.

Arda Vague told the party that Brother Hogbeard's zealots are handing out their own rough justice, and that the Berkmaster doesn't really do much for Dreg anymore. The real power may be Madam Shantywood, who runs the Shantywood Isles and a manor house of ill-repute.

A letter found with Grognott's map mentions Rodger Burl, a member of the Dreg Constabulary being paid by Grognot's boss to turn a blind eye to certain happenings within Dreg.

From Dreebe: Dreg used to be a nice town and a real business hub. The fish from Dreg supplies the woods and the river Hameth provides trade traffic. But lately it has become very unkind. People squabble, lots of violence and petty theft.

The party discussed Grognott's map over lunch at The House of the Harridwn and realised that Scrag and Bert are not just names of the killed Crockhorns but names of different spots on the map. The Crockhorns travelled down from the north, headed to Droomen Knoll, but their final destination was Dreg. There must be a connection between Dreg's recent troubles and what the Crockhorns were up to.

Inn recommendations from Mallowyn and Trellayde Occland: - The Mermaid's Arms: nice spot, though locals call it the Mermaid's Arse - Shogerty's Shack in the port: avoid it - Madam Shantywood's place across the Hameth river: accessed by shuttle boat
